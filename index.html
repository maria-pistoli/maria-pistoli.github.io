    StrokeLens

    A stroke screening aid built on the FAST signs.

    A short spoken test of face, arms and voice that says call 166 / 112 when a sign holds. Camera and audio never leave the computer.

    • 0.94AUC, face asymmetry
    • 0.85AUC, speech
    • 0.75AUC, arm drift

    Per-subject results on public datasets. A screening aid, not a diagnosis.

    • MediaPipe
    • Speech analysis
    • Python · OpenCV
